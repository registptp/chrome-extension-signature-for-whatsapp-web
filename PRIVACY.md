# Privacy Policy for Signature for WhatsApp Web

**Effective date:** May 20, 2026

Signature for WhatsApp Web is built as a free, open-source tool. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
This extension **does not collect, store, log, or share** any personal user data, messages, phone numbers, or identifying information with external servers. 

### 2. Local Storage
All user preferences (such as your customized signature text and the extension toggle state) are stored securely and locally on your own device using Chrome's native storage synchronization (`chrome.storage.sync`). 

### 3. External Links and Donations
The extension interface provides optional links to external platforms (such as LinkedIn, PayPal, and Pix QR Code) to allow users to connect or support the developer voluntarily. When you click these links, you will be redirected to external websites governed by their own privacy policies.

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer.
